# Tariff Classification & Origin Refund Recovery

Identify duty refunds through corrected classification, origin qualification, exclusions, and timely customs protests.

**Primary buyer:** Importers and customs compliance teams. **Evidence:** product catalogs, bills of material, HTS classifications, country of origin, trade agreements, entries, duties, rulings, protests, and refunds.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Product master ingestion
- HTS classification review
- Binding ruling library
- Country-of-origin analysis
- Substantial transformation test
- Trade agreement qualification
- Section 301 exclusion mapping
- Entry line ingestion
- Duty recalculation
- Post-summary correction
- Protest deadline control
- Prior disclosure coordination
- Refund evidence package
- Customs response tracking
- Product origin analytics
